About BlueTrace

Every dive leaves a trace.

The question is what kind of trace we choose to leave behind.

Our Story

The idea behind BlueTrace began beneath the ocean’s surface.

During our diving experiences, we witnessed plastic waste drifting through the water and coral reefs showing signs of stress and degradation. We realized that while many divers deeply care about the ocean, few understand how everyday choices — from sunscreen use to equipment habits — can leave invisible traces on marine ecosystems.

This experience inspired us to create BlueTrace, a platform that transforms underwater observations and scientific knowledge into an interactive learning journey.

Who We Are

BlueTrace is a student-led ocean awareness project developed by a team of high school researchers, designers and divers united by a passion to understand and protect marine ecosystems.

Our Mission

To empower divers with scientific and practical knowledge for more responsible ocean exploration.

Through interactive experiences, we aim to help ocean lovers understand their impact and make choices that better protect the ecosystems they admire.

Every dive leaves a trace. The question is what kind of trace we choose to leave behind.

Our Approach

Built within 24 hours using Vibe Coding, BlueTrace combines diver observations, environmental research, AI-assisted development, and interactive design to transform marine science into an immersive experience.

By connecting human choices with visible changes in coral ecosystems, we turn complex environmental issues into a story that divers can see, understand, and act upon.

Our Vision

We envision a future where responsible diving becomes part of ocean exploration — where every diver is not only an observer, but also a protector of the marine environments they love.
